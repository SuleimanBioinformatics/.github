## 🔬 Welcome to SuleimanBioinformatics

**SuleimanBioinformatics** is a centralized open-science organization dedicated to advancing computational genomics, macromolecular biophysics, artificial intelligence in structural biology, and mathematical foundations of machine learning.

We bridge theoretical rigor from **Cambridge University's Mathematics of Machine Learning** with practical, high-throughput genomic data pipelines and modern in-browser biocomputational visualizations.

---

## 🗂️ Primary Repositories

### 1. 🌐 [SuleimanBioinformatics.github.io](https://github.com/SuleimanBioinformatics/SuleimanBioinformatics.github.io)
> **The Official Frontend Web Portal & Interactive Biophysics Suite**  
> 🔗 **Live Website:** [https://suleimanbioinformatics.github.io/](https://suleimanbioinformatics.github.io/)

* **Genomic GC-Skew Analyzer:** Real-time sliding window calculator ($S_{GC} = \frac{C - G}{C + G}$) with interactive SVG vector plots for replication origin identification.
* **Kyte-Doolittle Hydropathy Profile:** Live peptide hydropathy scoring with configurable window radii and transmembrane threshold indicators (+1.6 cutoff).
* **3D Biomolecular Structure Viewer:** Client-side 3D Canvas visualizer rendering macromolecular coordinates (Insulin 1COH, Alpha-helices, DNA double helix) with interactive orbit rotation and zoom.
* **Curricula & Knowledge Explorer:** Dynamic real-time search across 30 Biopython modules, Cambridge ML lectures, and 50+ research wiki syntheses.

---

### 2. 🧬 [SuleimanBioinformatics](https://github.com/SuleimanBioinformatics/SuleimanBioinformatics)
> **The Core Backend Hub, Datasets, Algorithmic Starter Kits & Knowledge Graph**

* **30-Module Biopython Curriculum:** Systematic roadmap from `Bio.Seq` operations, remote NCBI Entrez queries, Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA), to `Bio.PDB` macromolecular structures.
* **Cambridge ML Mathematics Series:** Lecture notes, mathematical proofs, and R implementations covering regularized estimation ($L_1$ vs. $L_2$), bias-variance tradeoff, AdaBoost, and gradient boosting trees.
* **Computational Biophysics:** Agent-based ecosystem simulations (WaTor), predator-prey dynamics, and cellular automata.
* **Algorithmic Starter Kits:** Python production scripts for GC-skew windowing, Kyte-Doolittle profiles, and AlphaFold PDB coordinate & Miyazawa-Jernigan contact energy parsing.
* **Obsidian LLM Wiki Vault:** 50+ interlinked dossiers connecting concepts (DESeq2, ChIP-Seq, Kaplan-Meier, CUB), tools (BLAST, Nextflow), and benchmark targets.
