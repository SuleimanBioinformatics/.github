## About SuleimanBioinformatics

SuleimanBioinformatics is a research group and computational biology initiative focused on high-throughput sequence analysis, macromolecular biophysics, and mathematical modeling in machine learning.

The work combines practical bioinformatics workflows with statistical learning theory based on the University of Cambridge mathematics curriculum.

---

## Primary Repositories

### 1. [SuleimanBioinformatics.github.io](https://github.com/SuleimanBioinformatics/SuleimanBioinformatics.github.io)
**Frontend Web Portal and In-Browser Tools**  
Website: [https://suleimanbioinformatics.github.io/](https://suleimanbioinformatics.github.io/)

* **Genomic GC-Skew Analyzer:** Sliding-window calculation of GC-skew ($S_{GC} = \frac{C - G}{C + G}$) to identify bacterial replication origins (*oriC*).
* **Kyte-Doolittle Hydropathy Profile:** Amino acid hydropathy evaluation across sliding windows for transmembrane helix detection (+1.6 threshold).
* **3D Biomolecular Structure Viewer:** Client-side 3D Canvas visualizer for macromolecular coordinates (including Insulin 1COH, alpha-helices, and DNA structures) with interactive rotation and zoom.
* **Curricula and Documentation Explorer:** Searchable index covering Biopython modules, Cambridge machine learning lectures, and research concept dossiers.

---

### 2. [SuleimanBioinformatics](https://github.com/SuleimanBioinformatics/SuleimanBioinformatics) (Private Repository)
**Core Datasets, Algorithmic Scripts, Curricula, and Research Notes**  
Access Status: Private. For access requests, email [suleyman.hacizade1@gmail.com](mailto:suleyman.hacizade1@gmail.com?subject=Access%20Request%20-%20SuleimanBioinformatics%20Private%20Repository).

* **Biopython Curriculum (30 Modules):** Structured progression covering `Bio.Seq` operations, remote NCBI Entrez queries, multiple sequence alignment (`Bio.AlignIO`), and macromolecular structure modeling (`Bio.PDB`).
* **Cambridge Machine Learning Series:** Lecture notes, mathematical proofs, and R implementations covering regularized estimation ($L_1$ vs. $L_2$), bias-variance tradeoff, AdaBoost, and gradient boosting trees.
* **Computational Biophysics:** Agent-based ecosystem simulations (WaTor), predator-prey dynamics, and cellular automata.
* **Starter Scripts:** Python scripts for GC-skew sliding windows, hydropathy profiles, and PDB coordinate parsing with Miyazawa-Jernigan contact potentials.
* **Research Documentation:** Interlinked notes covering differential gene expression (DESeq2), ChIP-Seq analysis, survival analysis, and codon usage bias.
